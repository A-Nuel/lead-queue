# Design direction — Nuel Intelligence

Research operations console, not a generic startup landing page.

Palette: ink #080D16, surface #111B2A, border #253449, text #E7EDF7, cyan #19B6C8.
Typography: restrained sans-serif, high-contrast hierarchy, tabular metrics.
Navigation: research operations tabs, evidence and status are more important than decorative charts.
Interactions: no meaningless animation; errors and empty states explain the next action.

Inspired by the design critique process in anthropics/skills frontend-design and Vercel web-interface guidelines. This is a first styling pass on Streamlit, not a polished custom frontend. A real browser screenshot and accessibility review are still required.
